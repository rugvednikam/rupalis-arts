Meet the Maker

"Every stitch carries a prayer for your home's happiness."

"When I started Rupali's Arts, my dream was simple: replace artificial chemical powders that get swept away in an hour with timeless woolen art that families treasure for every festival."

Crafted in our family home in Maharashtra, each piece uses premium high-twist wool yarn and plush micro-velvet. Whether you are welcoming Ganpati Bappa home or illuminating your doorstep for Diwali, we make your celebrations mess-free and unforgettable.

Smart Festive Living

Traditional Powder vs Rupali's Woolen Rangoli

Experience the joy of traditional beauty with modern convenience and sustainable craftsmanship.

Traditional Chemical Powder

Powder Rangoli

  • Takes 1–2 hours to draw meticulously
  • Smudges with ceiling fans, foot traffic, or pets
  • Messy cleanup; swept and discarded in 1 day
  • Synthetic chemicals & harsh coloring dyes
  • Single-use expense every single festival
✨ Rupali's Arts Handcrafted

Woolen & Velvet Rangoli

  • Instant 2-second setup on any floor or pooja thali
  • Wind-proof, fan-proof & non-slip sturdy base
  • 100% washable — gently handwash and lay flat
  • Skin-safe, soft colorfast premium woolen yarn
  • Reusable for 5+ years across Diwali, Ganpati & weddings
