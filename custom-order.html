Bespoke & Custom Sizing

Every altar and home is unique. We tailor the diameter, petal colors, and layout of our woolen rangolis and Ganpati aasans to fit your exact deity dimensions and festive themes.

Altar Fitting Guide

Choosing the Right Idol Aasan Size

Measure the base of your deity idol or pooja platform. Here is our recommended size guide:

10" Standard Small

Fits Idols: 4" – 6" height

Perfect for daily home mandirs, Kalash sthapana, and compact altar shelves.

12" Popular Choice

Fits Idols: 7" – 9" height

Ideal standard size for eco-friendly Clay Ganpati idols and Diwali Laxmi Pooja.

14" Grand Altar

Fits Idols: 10" – 13" height

Spacious plush seating throne with layered velvet petals for prominent deity setups.

16" – 24"+ Bespoke Statement

Fits Idols: 14"+ height

Custom-crafted for society pandals, large hall altars, wedding mandaps, and corporate entries.
