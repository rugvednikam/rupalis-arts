Washing & Maintenance

How to Wash & Care for Woolen Rangolis

Our woolen rangolis are crafted from high-grade colorfast wool yarn that retains its vibrant colors for years when cared for properly.

1. Gentle Handwash

Dissolve a few drops of mild liquid detergent in cold water. Swirl the rangoli gently with your hands for 1–2 minutes. Avoid harsh scrubbing, brushing, or wringing.

2. Lay Flat to Dry in Shade

Gently press between two clean towels to absorb excess moisture. Lay flat on a surface in shade or indirect sunlight. Do not hang with clips to preserve circular shape.

3. Flat Festive Storage

Once completely dry, store flat in the protective breathable cotton pouch or box provided by Rupali's Arts. Ready to bloom again for next festival!
