Our Story & The Handcrafted Art

How a passion for Indian festive traditions and woolen handcraft in Maharashtra grew into a beloved artisanal home studio.

🧶
Hand-Knitted with Devotion Family craft from Maharashtra
The Beginning

Crafting Memories That Last Beyond One Festival Day

"Rangoli has always been our way of inviting deity blessings and welcoming guests. But sweeping away synthetic chemical powder hours later always felt heartbreaking. I wanted to create something families could cherish year after year."

Rupali's Arts started right at our living room table in Maharashtra. What began as handmade woolen mandala mats and velvet seating aasans for our own Ganpati Bappa quickly caught the eyes of friends and neighbors.

Today, every single piece is handcrafted with love to order. We source high-grade, fast-color wool yarn and plush micro-velvets, ensuring each piece is vibrant, washable, and built to remain in family homes for 5+ years.

Artisanal Journey

The 4-Step Crafting Process

From raw, vibrant woolen yarn skeins to a finished festive centerpiece, discover how every piece comes to life.

01

Yarn Selection

We handpick premium, high-twist colorfast wool yarn in traditional festive shades — marigold orange, golden yellow, ruby crimson, and emerald green.

02

Concentric Tufting

Yarn is intricately looped, braided, and knotted onto a soft, durable backing, creating dense, mess-free mandalas with rich tactile texture.

03

Velvet & Pearl Detailing

For our sunflower Ganpati aasans and diya holders, plush micro-velvet petals and delicate faux pearls & kundan stones are hand-stitched one by one.

04

Eco-Friendly Presentation

Every finished piece undergoes quality inspection, gentle smoothing, and is wrapped in neat protective packaging ready for display.
